Platform Architecture

Explore the live technical demonstration of our B2B SaaS engine.

1 Enterprise Portal

Where a bank or NGO registers their organization and generates a secure API key to access the Transcribe'Her engine.

2 Form Builder

The no-code dashboard where organizations dynamically add custom questions in Sidaamu Afoo and save them to our database.

3 Institution View

The end-user experience. This pulls the dynamic forms from our database. Users press "play" to hear the question and "speak" to fill it out.

4 Developer Playground

A public sandbox where developers can test the raw Text-to-Speech and Speech-to-Text API endpoints for any use case.
