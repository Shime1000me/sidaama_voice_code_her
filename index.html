B2B Voice Infrastructure

Bridging the literacy gap through voice.

Transcrib.her is the API infrastructure powering Sidaamu Afoo speech recognition and synthesis. We don't build the apps—we power the banks and NGOs that serve unbanked communities.

The Problem

Millions of women in rural Ethiopia face a severe literacy barrier. They are highly knowledgeable but cannot read or write formal documents. This locks them out of microfinance, healthcare, and digital banking, often leading to shame at service counters.

Our Solution

We built a voice-first AI engine for low-resource languages. Organizations use our API to let users speak their answers instead of typing. Our AI transcribes the voice and fills the forms automatically. No reading required.

Platform Architecture

We've built a complete B2B SaaS platform. Explore the live technical demonstration below.

🏢 1. Enterprise Portal

Where a bank or NGO registers their organization and generates a secure API key to access the Transcrib.her engine.

🛠️ 2. Form Builder (Admin)

The no-code dashboard where organizations dynamically add custom questions in Sidaamu Afoo and save them to our database.

📱 3. Institution View (Live)

The end-user experience. This pulls the dynamic forms from our database. Users press "play" to hear the question and "speak" to fill it out.

🎙️ 4. Developer Playground

A public sandbox where developers can test the raw Text-to-Speech and Speech-to-Text API endpoints for any use case.
